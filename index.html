Choosing the Right Paint for Humid Dubai Interiors

Not every paint suits every wall. For living areas and bedrooms, a quality matt or silk emulsion gives a smooth, soft look and cleans easily. Kitchens, bathrooms and poorly ventilated rooms need anti-mould, moisture-resistant formulas, because steam and humidity can cause bubbling and dark spots on standard paint. Ceilings look best in a flat white that hides small flaws, while doors and skirting need a harder satin or gloss finish that stands up to knocks and cleaning.

Low-odour and low-VOC paints are worth considering for bedrooms, clinics and any space people will use soon after painting. We explain the options in plain language during the site visit and show you sample colours on your own walls, because colours always look different in Naif's natural light than they do on a screen or a shade card.

Why Preparation Decides How Long Paint Lasts

Most paint failures start under the paint. In older Naif buildings we often find hairline cracks, uneven plaster, flaking layers from earlier jobs and damp patches near windows or pipes. Painting over these only hides the problem for a few months. Our crews scrape loose paint, repair cracks, skim uneven areas, sand smooth and apply the correct primer before the first colour coat. This extra step is what gives walls a flat, even finish and keeps colour from peeling or yellowing.

We also protect your property while we work. Furniture is moved and covered, floors are sheeted, sockets and fittings are masked and the area is cleaned at the end of every day. When the job is done, we walk through each room with you and touch up anything that is not perfect.

What Affects Painting Cost and Timeline in Naif

The main cost drivers are the total wall and ceiling area, ceiling height, the condition of the surfaces, the paint quality and the number of coats. How much does interior painting cost in Naif, Dubai?

Cost depends on the wall and ceiling area, the condition of the surfaces, the paint quality and finish you choose, and how much repair work is needed. We give a free, itemised quote after a short site visit or a photo and video review.

How long does interior painting take?

A single room can often be finished in one to two days, while a full apartment usually takes several days, including preparation, primer, two coats and drying time. Larger shops and offices are scheduled around your opening hours.

Do you move and protect furniture during painting?

Yes. We move furniture to the centre of the room, cover floors and items with protective sheets, mask sockets and fittings, and clean the area when the job is finished.
